About Memory Postcard

Memory Postcard transforms your cherished personal memories into illustrated vintage postcards using generative AI on AWS.

  • Amazon Bedrock Nova Lite: Analyzes your text to craft an evocative title, short poem, poignant quote, and image generation prompt.
  • Amazon Bedrock Nova Canvas: Renders a high-quality 1152x768 artwork matching your requested aesthetic style.
  • AWS Lambda & S3: Serverless backend hosting, generating 7-day presigned URLs for instant sharing and privacy.
