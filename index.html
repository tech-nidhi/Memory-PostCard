About Postcards from Elsewhere

Postcards from Elsewhere is an always-on autonomous creative AI agent built on AWS. Every day, the system autonomously imagines a new destination and delivers a unique digital postcard directly to your mailbox.

  • 10 Immersive Scenic Destinations: Rainy Day, Snowy Morning, Sunset Escape, Autumn Path, Mountain Calling, Cozy Evening, Vintage Vibes, City Lights, Watercolor Dream, Monsoon Memories.
  • Rich Environment Prompts: Describes weather, lighting, props, and camera optics in Amazon Bedrock Nova Canvas.
  • Dynamic Stamps & Cancellation Postmarks: Theme-matched postal graphics rendered dynamically.
